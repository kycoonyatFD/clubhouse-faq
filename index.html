What is Clubhouse?
FanDuel Clubhouse is the company's first-ever internal contest simulating the sportsbook experience. It allows employees worldwide to engage with and provide feedback on the core sportsbook product in a 100% legal and compliant environment — deepening product knowledge in a fun, competitive way.
Employee Eligibility
Clubhouse is available to all current, exempt salary full-time FanDuel employees under a permanent or fixed-term agreement who are 21+ and issued Clubhouse accounts. This includes employees in: the US, Canada, UK, Porto (Technology/Product/Data/Enterprise teams), Dublin (FanDuel-specific job titles), and Australia. Any US employee on a state gambling exclusion list is not eligible.
Compliance Rules
⚠️ Employee Play Policy — Must Follow
  • Bets must be placed using an employee FanDuel Clubhouse account only
  • Winnings may NOT be withdrawn from Clubhouse accounts
  • Personal funds may NOT be deposited to Clubhouse accounts
  • Funds from other FanDuel accounts may NOT be transferred to Clubhouse
  • Clubhouse login may NOT be used on other FanDuel products (Sportsbook, DFS)
  • Violating these rules may result in disciplinary action
Contest Period & Entry
The contest period is announced via #internal-contests Slack and displayed in the Promotions Carousel. Eligible employees are automatically issued site credit and/or Bonus Bets at the start of each contest. Additional generosity may include Profit Boost Tokens, Boost Builder Tokens, and No Sweat Tokens (valid for cash wagers only, not Bonus Bets).
Contests typically run once a month, usually around tentpole sporting events. Check #internal-contests for exact start/end dates.
Leaderboards
Not all leaderboards are available for each contest — check the contest announcement in #internal-contests.
🏆 Largest Final Cash Balance
Employees with the largest overall cash balance at the end of the Contest Period, regardless of wager type, event, or stake amount.
🎯 Biggest Odds on a Winning Single Wager
Biggest odds on a winning Single Wager with an initial stake of at least $1.00. "Biggest odds" = odds with the largest numerical value. Single wagers = standard straight wagers (moneyline, spread, over/under, individual player props). Parlays and futures are excluded. Wagers must settle to completion — no cashouts.
💰 Biggest Return on Investment (Single Wager)
Biggest net winnings (total payout minus initial stake) on a winning Single Wager with a stake of at least $1.00.
📊 YourWay Leaderboard
Employees who make the most cumulative money betting via the YourWay tab. All winnings from YourWay bets are summed at the end of the contest.

Bonus Bets & Reward Tokens
What is a Bonus Bet? ▼

A bonus bet is a risk-free wager that needs to "win" to be converted into cash. The stake is not returned — only net winnings.

Example: $100 bonus bet at -200 odds → if you win, you receive $50 in cash (not the $100 stake). If you lose, the bonus bet is gone.

Leaderboards are based on cash balance, not bonus bet balance. If you use your entire bonus accidentally, we cannot void those wagers.

Profit Boost Tokens (PBT) ▼

PBTs increase the odds of a bet by a predetermined percentage. Example: +393 odds with a 30% PBT → odds increase to +510.

Toggle appears on your betslip for eligible wagers. Valid for cash bets only. Cannot be combined with Bonus Bets.

Boost Builder Tokens (BBT) ▼

BBTs apply to parlays. A 3-leg parlay gets a 15% boost. Each additional leg (up to 12) increases the boost by 10 percentage points.

Toggle appears on your betslip for eligible parlays. Valid for cash bets only.

🔑 Passkeys Beta — iOS Only (Android / Web coming soon)
FanDuel is rolling out passkey authentication to internal employees to test before releasing to customers. Passkeys are available on the Clubhouse iOS app only (iOS 16+, iCloud Keychain required).
What Are Passkeys?
Passkeys are a modern, passwordless way to sign in using cryptographic key pairs and your device's authentication (Face ID, Touch ID, passcode). Benefits: faster login, phishing-resistant, no passwords to remember, built-in MFA (something you have + something you are).

Passkeys FAQ
What if my biometric fails? ▼
You can still log in using your username and password.
Can I set up multiple passkeys? ▼
Yes, currently no limit. This will change prior to customer launch.
What if I get a new phone? ▼
If you use a synced password manager, your passkey should automatically be available on your new phone once you sign into your password manager.
Are passkeys required? ▼
No — passkeys are optional. You can continue using email and password to sign in.
My device isn't prompting me to use a passkey. Why? ▼
Check: iOS 16+ / macOS 13+, biometrics or PIN enabled, iCloud Keychain enabled. If your device doesn't support passkeys, email/password sign-in still works.

About the Clubhouse Support Model
Clubhouse is an educational, non-commercially-regulated state. It has a different support model from standard regulated states. Single-user account/access issues are typically not logged as incidents — issues with the app or service itself are logged via Service Management as normal incident management tickets.
If an issue is found to be more widespread than Clubhouse, it is escalated to ITOPs following the existing support model.

Access & Setup
What is Clubhouse? ▼
FanDuel Clubhouse is the company's first-ever internal contest simulating the sportsbook experience. It allows employees worldwide to engage with our core sportsbook product in a 100% legal, compliant environment — deepening product knowledge competitively and providing feedback.
How do I join Clubhouse? ▼
New employee accounts are typically created every 6 weeks, provisioned automatically via HRIS/Okta sync during onboarding. You'll be notified with your account details when created. Porto, Cluj, and Flutter org employees without Dayforce profiles can request access via the Atlassian Service Desk Portal (Runway → Clubhouse).
What will my Clubhouse email look like? ▼
US/UK/Porto: firstname.lastname+fdclubhouse@fanduel.com · Dublin: firstname.lastname+fdclubhouse@ppb.com · Australia: firstname.lastname+fdclubhouse@sportsbet.com.au. Check the accounts spreadsheet (linked in Resources) if unsure.
How do I log in for the first time? ▼
Go to clubhouse.sportsbook.fanduel.com → click "Forgot your password?" → enter your +fdclubhouse email → check your normal FanDuel work inbox for the reset email → click the first link → set a new password. When prompted for DOB and SSN, use DOB: 01/01/1990 and SSN: 9999.
I don't have Runway on my Okta. What do I do? ▼
Open the Atlassian Service Desk Portal (on your Okta dashboard) → Access Request Board → Common Requests → Access Request → System: Runway → Permission: Runway - Clubhouse → explain you need it to play internal contests. Granted within a few days pending manager approval.
I get "You do not have permission to view this content" on Runway. ▼
On iPhone, make sure you're on a managed device. Managed device enrollment steps can be found in Confluence (search "BYOD enrollment"). Alternatively, play Clubhouse via Web — see the guide in the canvas at the top of #internal-contests. Or post in #clubhouse-support.
Compliance & Restrictions
Can I withdraw funds from Clubhouse? ▼
No. Withdrawing funds is against the employee play policy. Balances are cleared between contests.
Can I deposit personal funds? ▼
No. Depositing personal funds into a Clubhouse account is against the employee play policy.
Can I use my Clubhouse login on other FanDuel products? ▼
No. It's against the employee play policy to log in via Clubhouse on other FanDuel products (Sportsbook, DFS). Keep it to Clubhouse only.
Are there country restrictions? ▼
Yes — Clubhouse cannot be used in Romania due to local laws. If you get a 404 or blank page from a different country, try a VPN routing from the USA. If that doesn't work, post in #clubhouse-support.
Are markets limited on Clubhouse? ▼
No — the experience mirrors what customers have. Occasionally, new features will be tested on Clubhouse first. If you think a market is missing, post in #clubhouse-product-feedback. If you believe it's a bug, post in #clubhouse-support.
Contests & Prizes
How do Clubhouse contests work? ▼
1. Contest announced in #internal-contests and via email. 2. Contest funds auto-added to your account when it starts. 3. Use funds + promos + winnings to wager. 4. Leaderboard determines winners. 5. Results posted in #internal-contests. Awards via Spotlight (US/UK/CA), HR (Dublin/Porto/AUS).
How often do contests run? How long do they last? ▼
Typically once a month, usually tied to tentpole sporting events. Most run for about 1 week, but always check #internal-contests for the exact dates.
I get "no funds deducted" when placing a bet. ▼
The contest may not have started yet. Double-check #internal-contests to confirm you're past the start date.
What prizes can I win? When will I receive them? ▼
Prizes are distributed within 30 days of contest end via Spotlight (US/UK/CA), FanDuel Australia Rewards (AUS), or Flutter Kudos (Dublin/Porto). Prize amounts vary by leaderboard position and contest.
I accidentally used all my bonus bets. Can I get more? ▼
No — we cannot void wagers or add more funds. Bonus bets are not eligible for cashouts.
I received a message saying my winnings were taken out. Why? ▼
Clubhouse balances are cleared at the end of each contest. This is expected behaviour.
Can I use Clubhouse to test features outside of contest periods? ▼
Post in #clubhouse-support with details of what you'd be testing. The team will review and look to accommodate the request.
