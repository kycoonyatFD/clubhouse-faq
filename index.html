2. Password Reset Email Not Received Login
▼
  1. Confirm user is entering their +fdclubhouse email — firstname.lastname+fdclubhouse@fanduel.com
  2. Check the accounts spreadsheet — if not listed, their account hasn't been provisioned yet
  3. If not in spreadsheet, request an account via the ARB:
    • Access Request System: Runway
    • Permission Level: Runway - Clubhouse
  4. If they ARE in the spreadsheet but still not receiving the email: check for name changes (married name vs maiden name). The email can be updated to match
  5. As a workaround, generate and DM a direct password reset link

▼

To request an account, create a ticket on the ARB, asking for:

  • Access Request System: Runway
  • Permission Level: Runway - Clubhouse

This will generate an account for the user.

4. Location Verification Failed — Web Location
▼
  1. If the user's account is brand new — they may not have been whitelisted for GeoComply yet. Whitelisting happens in batches on Fridays. If it has been less than one Friday since they were hired, ask them to wait. If it has been more than one Friday, continue troubleshooting below.
  2. Disconnect from GlobalProtect VPN — the web version requires no VPN
  3. Check Netskope — confirm "Private Access" is enabled
  4. Clear browser cache and cookies, then reload
  5. If still failing, try a different browser
  6. If issue persists: switch to the mobile app as a workaround, and if this appears to be a wider issue (not user error), raise it via #sportsbook-support
If the user is outside the US, try routing traffic via VPN from a US server.
5. Location Verification Failed — Mobile App Location
▼
  1. If the user's account is brand new — they may not have been whitelisted for GeoComply yet. Whitelisting happens in batches on Fridays. If it has been less than one Friday since they were hired, ask them to wait. If more than one Friday has passed, continue below.
  2. Confirm user is logged in with their +fdclubhouse account, not their regular FanDuel account
  3. Check app location permissions: iOS → Settings → find the app → Location → set to Always
  4. If permissions were previously denied, delete and reinstall the app — allow location on first launch
6. iOS "Untrusted Developer" Error Login
▼
  1. Go to Settings → General → VPN & Device Management → FanDuel Inc.
  2. Tap "Trust Developer"
  3. Restart the phone when prompted
  4. App should work after restart
7. Okta / FastPass Not Working on Phone Login
▼
  1. Open the Okta Verify app → tap "Launch Dashboard"
  2. Scroll to bottom of listed apps → find Runway → tap it
  3. Log in with your regular @fanduel.com email

No Runway on Okta? Atlassian Service Desk → Access Request Board → Common Requests → Access Request → System: Runway → Permission: Runway - Clubhouse.
8. MFA / Verification Code Prompt on New Device Login
▼

MFA is turned off for everyone by default. The only time a verification code is triggered is the new device challenge when first logging in to a new build.

  1. When prompted for a verification code, make sure the user selects the "Email" option at the bottom of the screen — text and authenticator options will not work
  2. The code email may arrive in their "Other" folder — make sure they check there if they can't find it in their inbox

11. 403 Error Accessing the Website Location
▼

12. Bets Not Appearing in "My Bets" Bet Issues
▼
  1. Check Transaction History — if the deduction is there, the bet was placed successfully and the issue is display-only
  2. Note the Bet Reference number (format: fd:01jw...) from Transaction History
  3. Reassure the user: their balance is correct even if the bet doesn't display
  4. If you believe this is a widespread issue or not user error, raise it via #sportsbook-support or get IT-Ops to help find an owner
13. Leaderboard Balance Doesn't Match Account Balance Bet Issues
▼

Leaderboards throughout the contest are always directional and never final. The final leaderboard is published 1 day after the contest ends.

  1. Reassure the user: whatever balance they see on their app is what will count towards the leaderboard
  2. If the balance appears wrong after the final leaderboard has been published, escalate to Eric Milam or Ky Coony — do not contact them before the final leaderboard is out
14. "This Service Is Unavailable" Message on Login Login
▼

This error typically means the user is logging in with the wrong email on the wrong app.

  1. Confirm the user is using their +fdclubhouse email on the Clubhouse app — not their regular FanDuel email
  2. Likewise, confirm they are not trying to use their Clubhouse credentials on the regular FanDuel Sportsbook app
15. No Balance / Funds in My Account Bet Issues
▼

When a contest starts, users are given bonus bets, not cash — they don't appear automatically as a spendable balance.

  1. Go to the Rewards section in the app
  2. Add a selection to the betslip
  3. Toggle on the bonus bet from the betslip to apply it to the wager
Bonus bets must "win" to be converted to cash. The stake is not returned — only net winnings carry over.

What is Clubhouse?
FanDuel Clubhouse is the company's first-ever internal contest platform — a fully legal, compliant sportsbook environment for employees. It mirrors the real FanDuel sportsbook experience, letting staff place bets, compete on leaderboards, win prizes, and generate product feedback before changes reach customers.
Employee Eligibility
Available to all full-time FanDuel employees 21+ under permanent or fixed-term agreements in: US, Canada, UK, Porto (Tech/Product/Data/Enterprise), Dublin (FanDuel-specific job titles), and Australia. US employees on a state gambling exclusion list are not eligible.
Compliance Rules
⚠️ Employee Play Policy — Must Follow
  • Bets must be placed using a Clubhouse account only
  • Winnings may NOT be withdrawn
  • Personal funds may NOT be deposited
  • Funds from other FanDuel accounts may NOT be transferred to Clubhouse
  • Clubhouse login may NOT be used on other FanDuel products
  • Violations may result in disciplinary action
Contest Period & Entry
Announced via #internal-contests and in the Promotions Carousel. Eligible employees are automatically issued site credit and/or Bonus Bets at contest start. Additional generosity may include Profit Boost Tokens, Boost Builder Tokens, and No Sweat Tokens (valid for cash wagers only).
Leaderboards
🏆 Largest Final Cash Balance
Largest overall cash balance at end of Contest Period, regardless of wager type or stake.
🎯 Biggest Odds on a Winning Single Wager
Biggest odds on a winning Single Wager (moneyline, spread, over/under, player props — no parlays/futures) with stake ≥ $1.00. Must settle to completion, no cashouts.
💰 Biggest Return on Investment (Single Wager)
Biggest net winnings (total payout minus initial stake) on a winning Single Wager with stake ≥ $1.00.
📊 YourWay Leaderboard
Most cumulative money earned betting via the YourWay tab. All YourWay winnings summed at contest end.

Bonus Bets & Tokens
Bonus Bets▼

A risk-free wager that must "win" to convert to cash. Only net winnings are kept — stake is not returned. Example: $100 bonus at -200 odds → win = $50 cash, lose = $0. Leaderboards track cash balance only.

Profit Boost Tokens (PBT)▼

Boost odds by a set percentage. Example: +393 + 30% PBT = +510. Toggle on betslip. Cash bets only.

Boost Builder Tokens (BBT)▼

Boost parlay odds. 3-leg parlay = +15%; each additional leg (up to 12) adds 10pp. Cash bets only.

Support Model Overview
Clubhouse is an educational, non-commercially-regulated state with a different support model. Single-user account/access issues are typically not logged as incidents. App or service issues are logged via Service Management as normal IM tickets. If more widespread than Clubhouse, escalate to ITOPs.
Clubhouse issues should never be paged. They can be raised as IMs with a maximum priority of P3. If a contest issue is potentially contest-breaking, do your best to get attention on it as fast as possible.

Access & Setup
What is Clubhouse? ▼
FanDuel Clubhouse is the company's internal contest platform — a 100% legal, compliant sportsbook environment for employees to deepen product knowledge, provide feedback, and compete for prizes.
How do I join Clubhouse? ▼
Accounts are provisioned automatically via HRIS/Okta sync during onboarding. If you haven't been provisioned, create a ticket on the ARB: Access Request System: Runway, Permission Level: Runway - Clubhouse.
What will my Clubhouse email look like? ▼
US/UK/Porto: firstname.lastname+fdclubhouse@fanduel.com · Dublin: firstname.lastname+fdclubhouse@ppb.com · Australia: firstname.lastname+fdclubhouse@sportsbet.com.au. Check the accounts spreadsheet if unsure.
How do I log in for the first time? ▼
Go to clubhouse.sportsbook.fanduel.com → "Forgot your password?" → enter your +fdclubhouse email → check your normal FanDuel work inbox for the reset email → click the link → set a new password. When prompted for DOB and SSN, use DOB: 01/01/1990 and SSN: 9999.
I get "You do not have permission to view this content" on Runway. ▼
You need to request access. Create a ticket on the ARB:

• Access Request System: Runway
• Permission Level: Runway - Clubhouse
I don't have Runway on my Okta. ▼
Create a ticket on the ARB: Access Request System: Runway, Permission Level: Runway - Clubhouse. Granted within a few days pending manager approval.
Compliance & Restrictions
Can I withdraw or deposit funds? ▼
No. Both are against the employee play policy. Balances are cleared between contests.
Can I use my Clubhouse login on other FanDuel products? ▼
No. Against the employee play policy. Security rules block cross-product login in both directions.
Are there country restrictions? ▼
Yes — Clubhouse cannot be used in Romania due to local laws. If you get a 404 or blank page from another country, try a VPN routed from the US.
Are markets limited on Clubhouse? ▼
No — the experience mirrors customers'. New features may be tested on Clubhouse first. Market feedback goes to #clubhouse-product-feedback.
Contests & Prizes
How do Clubhouse contests work? ▼
1. Contest announced in #internal-contests and via email. 2. Contest funds auto-added at start. 3. Wager using funds, promos, and winnings. 4. Leaderboard determines winners. 5. Results posted in #internal-contests. Awards via Spotlight (US/UK/CA) or HR (Dublin/Porto/AUS).
How often do contests run? How long do they last? ▼
~Once a month, around major sporting events. Most run ~1 week. Always check #internal-contests for exact dates.
What prizes can I win? When will I get them? ▼
Prizes distributed within 30 days via Spotlight (US/UK/CA), FanDuel AU Rewards (AUS), or Flutter Kudos (Dublin/Porto). Amounts vary by leaderboard position and contest.
I accidentally used all my bonus bets. Can I get more? ▼
No — we cannot void wagers or add more funds. Bonus bets are not eligible for cashouts.
I received a message saying my winnings were taken out. Why? ▼
Clubhouse balances are cleared at the end of each contest — expected behaviour.

Confirm you're on the Clubhouse app and logged in with your +fdclubhouse email. On web: ensure Netskope "Private Access" is enabled and GlobalProtect VPN is off.

Email fdgcomplianceops@fanduel.com to resolve.
Android: Cannot download after enabling "install from unknown sources." ▼
File a ticket with IT via the Jarvis Slack app asking for help enabling the Clubhouse download with MDM enabled.
