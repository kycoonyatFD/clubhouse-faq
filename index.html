Channel Summary

Analysis spans the full #clubhouse-support history. The overwhelming majority of issues fall into two buckets: password reset failures and location/geo-verification errors. Account provisioning for new hires and Okta/MFA problems are a consistent third tier. Most issues have a known fix — this guide documents them so the support team can resolve in under 2 minutes.

1
🔑 Password Reset — "Details Don't Match Our Records"
🔴 Very High Account ▼
What users report"The details you entered don't match our records. Check them and try again or contact your Support Team." — appears during password reset when entering DOB and SSN.

Fix — try in order

  1. Enter DOB: 01/01/1990 and SSN: 9999
  2. If that fails, try SSN: 1111 (same DOB)
  3. If none of those work, admin should DM the user a direct password reset link — this bypasses the DOB/SSN step entirely
  4. Last resort: raise an EITSD ticket via Jarvis
Note: These are placeholder credentials — Clubhouse accounts are not linked to real personal data. The correct combo varies by account batch. Try all variants before escalating.
2
📧 Password Reset Email Not Received
🔴 Very High Account ▼
What users report"I clicked 'Forgot Password' using my Clubhouse email but I never received the reset email."

Checklist

  1. Confirm the user is entering their +fdclubhouse email — firstname.lastname+fdclubhouse@fanduel.com — not their regular FD email
  2. Check the accounts spreadsheet — if not listed, their account hasn't been provisioned yet
  3. If not in the spreadsheet → post in #clubhouse-support to get them added to the next batch
  4. If they ARE in the spreadsheet but not receiving email → check if their Slack display name has changed (e.g. married name). The Clubhouse email may need updating to match
  5. Also check: did they recently accept Terms & Conditions? If not, the account may not be fully active
  6. As a workaround, admin can generate and DM a direct password reset link
Reset emails can sometimes be delayed by several minutes. If the user is certain they're using the correct email and are in the spreadsheet, wait 5 min and try once more before escalating.
3
📋 Account Not in Spreadsheet / Not Yet Provisioned
🔴 Very High Account ▼
What users report"I can't find my name in the spreadsheet" / "I'm a new hire and don't have an account yet."

Fix

  1. Click the user's Slack profile to confirm their current FD email
  2. Search the accounts spreadsheet by that exact email
  3. If they're missing → post in #clubhouse-support — accounts are provisioned in batches tied to contest cycles
  4. New hires who joined mid-cycle may need to wait until the next batch. Let them know when the next contest starts
  5. New employees are whitelisted in batches on Fridays
Note: IT support cannot create Clubhouse accounts — only the internal Clubhouse team can. Direct IT-redirected users back to #clubhouse-support.
4
🌐 Location Verification Failed — Web
🔴 High Location ▼
What users report"Sports betting isn't allowed where you're located" / "Can't verify location" even after installing the GeoComply plugin and restarting multiple times.

Step-by-step fix

  1. Disconnect from GlobalProtect VPN — the web version typically works without the VPN. Try disconnecting first
  2. Open Netskope settings → confirm "Private Access" is enabled (toggling this off is a very common root cause)
  3. Clear browser cache and cookies fully, then reload clubhouse.sportsbook.fanduel.com
  4. Try a different browser (Chrome vs Safari vs Firefox)
  5. Try an incognito/private window
  6. If none of the above works → switch to the mobile app as a reliable workaround. Web geo-verification has known intermittent issues
  7. For deep investigation: post the user's FD user ID in #clubhouse-support — the team can pull GeoComply request logs
Recurring web issue: Even after a successful fix, some users see this return. The mobile app is consistently more stable for location verification. Recommend app as primary access method.
5
📱 Location Verification Failed — Mobile App
🔴 High Location ▼
What users reportApp says location can't be verified, or "sports betting isn't allowed where you're located" on the mobile app.

Step-by-step fix

  1. Check the account being used. The most common cause: user is logged in with their regular FanDuel account instead of their +fdclubhouse account. Log out and back in with firstname.lastname+fdclubhouse@fanduel.com
  2. Check app location permissions: iOS → Settings → search FD SBW RE → Location → set to "Always"
  3. If location was previously denied: delete and reinstall the app, and allow Location when prompted on first launch
  4. Confirm the user is physically located in a whitelisted region
  5. For Android: if you see a state selector on the app screen, you may have the wrong app (production SBK app instead of the Clubhouse build from Runway). Reinstall the correct build
The Clubhouse app should never show a US state selector. If it does, it's the wrong build or the production app was accidentally opened.
6
🔐 Okta / FastPass Not Working on Phone
🟡 High App/Device ▼
What users report"Okta not on device" when scanning the QR code / FastPass won't launch / stuck in endless Okta login loop.

Fix

  1. Open the Okta Verify app → tap "Launch Dashboard"
  2. Scroll to the bottom of the app list and find Runway → tap it
  3. Log in using your regular @fanduel.com email
  4. Once on the Runway homepage, you can download the Clubhouse app build
  5. If Runway repeatedly redirects back to Okta Verify setup screens → this is an Okta FastPass configuration issue (not Clubhouse). Have the user open an IT ticket via Jarvis: "Need help configuring Okta FastPass on my device"
Note: For new phones or new employees — the Okta Verify app must be fully configured before Runway is accessible. IT/Okta setup needs to happen first.
7
🍎 iOS "Untrusted Developer" / App Won't Install
🟡 Medium App/Device ▼
What users report"Untrusted Enterprise Developer" dialog when opening the app, or app closes immediately after install.

Fix

  1. Go to Settings → General → VPN & Device Management → FanDuel Inc.
  2. Tap "Trust FanDuel Inc."
  3. Restart your phone when prompted
  4. Re-open the Clubhouse app — it should now load normally
This is a standard iOS enterprise app trust step. Safe to use as a canned response — it applies to anyone downloading the app for the first time on a new device.

🔗 Linked / Duplicate Account Error
🟡 Medium Account ▼
What users report"You already have a linked account" / "Duplicate account found" when trying to log in.

Fix

  1. Confirm the exact email the user is trying: it should be firstname.lastname+fdclubhouse@fanduel.com
  2. Post in #clubhouse-support — a backend account deduplication is needed and can't be self-resolved
  3. Alternatively, email fdgcomplianceops@fanduel.com with the user's details and the duplicate account situation
  4. This cannot be self-resolved by the user or most responders; it requires admin backend access
9
🎲 Bets Not Appearing in "My Bets"
🟡 Medium Bets ▼
What users report"I placed a bet and my balance decreased, but the bet doesn't appear under Open or Settled bets."

Fix

  1. Check Transaction History — if the balance deduction is there, the bet was placed correctly and this is a display-only issue
  2. Collect the Bet Reference number from Transaction History (format: fd:01jw...)
  3. Post the BetRef number(s) in #clubhouse-support for manual investigation and leaderboard correction
  4. Reassure the user: their balance is correct and bets aren't lost — it's a display/pipeline sync issue
Known issue: This has occurred during high-volume contest windows. Multiple users can be affected simultaneously. A channel-wide acknowledgment is typically posted in #clubhouse-support when this happens at scale.
10
🔒 Account Deactivated or Locked
🟡 Medium Account ▼
What users report"Your account has been deactivated" / "Contact customer support re: your account status. You're currently unable to reset your password."

Fix

  1. Email fdgcomplianceops@fanduel.com — this is the correct team to reactivate Clubhouse accounts. Include the user's +fdclubhouse email and a brief description
  2. Once ComplianceOps reactivates the account, the user will receive a password reset email and can proceed normally
  3. If the account was deactivated immediately after creation (within minutes), post in #clubhouse-support — this may be a provisioning issue rather than a compliance one
Note: Employees who left and rejoined FanDuel commonly hit this. ComplianceOps handles reactivation end-to-end.

💬 MFA / 6-Digit SMS Code Not Received
🟢 Low-Medium Account ▼
What users report"Login asks for a 6-digit code sent to my phone but I never receive it." / "Resend by email says 'We couldn't resend your code.'"

Fix

  1. Wait 60 seconds and try "Resend" once more — SMS delivery can lag
  2. Check if there's an email verification option at the bottom of the verification screen and use that instead
  3. If the code is consistently going to an old/wrong phone number → an admin needs to update the MFA number. Open an EITSD ticket via Jarvis: "Need to update MFA phone number on Clubhouse account"
  4. Got a new phone? Same process — the old MFA config needs resetting by IT

🏆 Leaderboard Balance Doesn't Match Account / Not Appearing
🟢 Low-Medium Bets ▼
What users report"My leaderboard balance is different from my in-app balance" / "I'm not appearing on the leaderboard at all despite having a balance."

Fix

  1. The in-app account balance is always the source of truth — leaderboard data is derived and can lag
  2. The leaderboard requires at least one placed wager before a user appears
  3. Post the user's name and balance in #clubhouse-support for manual leaderboard correction
  4. No action needed on the user's side — their bets and funds are safe
Known issue: The singles leaderboard in particular has had recurring accuracy issues. Channel-wide notices are posted in #clubhouse-support when this is actively being fixed.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is my Clubhouse email address? ▼
Your Clubhouse email follows the format firstname.lastname+fdclubhouse@fanduel.com. It's different from your regular FD work email — the +fdclubhouse suffix designates it as your internal Clubhouse account. You can verify it in the accounts spreadsheet.
Do I need to be on the VPN to use Clubhouse? ▼
For the web version — generally no. Being on GlobalProtect VPN often causes location verification to fail. Disconnect from VPN first. However, Netskope "Private Access" should remain enabled. For the mobile app — VPN is not required and shouldn't affect it.
What state do I select during setup / password reset? ▼
During password reset, select any US state — it doesn't matter which one. Clubhouse is an internal tool and not restricted by real geographic state laws. If you're outside the US (e.g. Canada, Australia, UK), just pick any state and it will work. If the app is showing you a state selector on the main screen (not during setup), that likely means you have the wrong build — see Issue #5.
Can I use Clubhouse on my personal phone? ▼
Yes — you no longer need to enrol your personal phone in BYOD/MDM to use Clubhouse. You can download the app directly from Runway on your personal device. Follow the same steps in the main canvas download guide.
Can I withdraw or cash out my Clubhouse winnings? ▼
Winnings from internal Clubhouse contests (Big Dog, Mad Dog, etc.) are not real-money withdrawals in the traditional sense — prizes are distributed separately by the contest organisers. For questions about specific prize redemption, ask in #clubhouse-support. For any withdrawal from a linked personal FanDuel account, contact fdgcomplianceops@fanduel.com.
How does the leaderboard work? ▼
The leaderboard reflects your current account balance (winnings + remaining starting funds). You won't appear on the leaderboard until you've placed at least one bet. The singles/straight bets leaderboard is a separate view that tracks best individual bet odds won — this one in particular has had known sync delays. Your in-app balance is always the accurate figure.
I'm a new hire — when will my Clubhouse account be created? ▼
Accounts are provisioned in batches, typically on Fridays and ahead of each new contest cycle. If you've just joined and the contest is already running, you may miss the current one. Post in #clubhouse-support to confirm your timeline and get added to the next batch.
Can interns participate in Clubhouse? ▼
This depends on the current contest rules. Check the official gameplay rules or post in #clubhouse-support — eligibility can vary by contest and region.
The Android app keeps breaking — what should I do? ▼
Android builds have historically had intermittent issues (broken builds, state selector appearing, "service unavailable" errors). First, try downloading a different build version from Runway — sometimes the newest build has a bug and an older build works. If all builds fail, use the web version at clubhouse.sportsbook.fanduel.com in the meantime. Post in #clubhouse-support if multiple people are affected — it may be a broader build issue.
Can I place round robins or parlays? ▼
Parlays are supported in Clubhouse. Round robins may be available depending on the current build and markets. Not all parlay combinations are allowed — the bet slip will notify you if two selections can't be combined. There's no way to know in advance which legs are incompatible before adding them.
What is my starting balance and how does it count on the leaderboard? ▼
Contest formats vary, but typically you'll receive a starting cash balance (e.g. $50) and/or bonus bets. Your leaderboard position reflects your total current balance (remaining funds + winnings). You won't appear on the leaderboard until you've placed a wager. Unplaced starting funds alone don't put you on the board.
Can I cash out a bet I placed? ▼
Cash out is available on some markets where the option appears on your open bet. However, it may not be available on all bet types or after certain in-game triggers. If you see the cash out button but it's unresponsive, try restarting the app. If a bet needs to be voided for other reasons (e.g. incorrect game selected), raise it in #clubhouse-support — these are handled case by case.
I'm based in Dublin / Porto / Australia — can I access Clubhouse? ▼
Yes, Clubhouse is available to employees globally. However:
• Dublin/UKI employees should use their @fanduel.com email, not @uki.fanduel.com or @paddypowerbetfair.com
• For the state selector during setup, choose any US state — this is cosmetic and doesn't restrict access
• For SSN, use the placeholder 9999 — international employees don't have US SSNs
• Australia (Sportsbet) users follow the same flow with their +fdclubhouse@sportsbet.com.au email format
I can't access the Confluence game play rules page — what do I do? ▼
The Confluence rules page requires access to the NSL Confluence space. If you get an "access denied" error, request access through your manager or IT. In the meantime, the key rules are summarised in the Clubhouse FAQ page.
